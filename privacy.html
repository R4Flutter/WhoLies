Privacy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

The short version

Who's Lying? doesn't ask for your email or an account. Your scores and streak are saved in your own browser. Only if you join the leaderboard are your chosen name and scores shared.

What the game stores on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage to remember your progress: today's answers, your streak, your stats, your endless-mode best and whether sound is on. This never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data resets it.

Advertising

This site may show ads from Google AdSense to keep the game free. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to show ads and measure them, based on your visits to this and other websites. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and Switzerland), you'll be asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and turn off personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits and see which parts of the game people use, such as how many cases are finished. This data is grouped and isn't used to identify you.

The leaderboard

Joining the leaderboard is optional. If you join, the game signs you in with a random, anonymous ID (no email or password) and stores the detective name you choose with your daily scores and your best endless run. Your name and scores are shown publicly on the leaderboard. This data is kept on Google Firebase. Pick a name that doesn't identify you if you'd rather stay private.

Sharing your result

When you tap Share, the game creates a short text with your score. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to paste or send it.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@whoslying.example.
