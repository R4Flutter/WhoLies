              How to play

              The police checked some facts. They're true. Then three suspects tell you their story.

              One story can't be true. Compare each one with the facts and tap the liar.

              Twist cases

              Every 5th case changes the rules. Sometimes two people are lying and you must find the honest one. Sometimes everyone is honest and one fact is fake.

              Stars

              • First try, no hint, under the target time

              Modes

              Daily Case: 5 cases a day, the same for everyone, one try each. Career: chapter after chapter of cases. Rush: catch as many liars as you can before you run out of lives. Night Shift: one hard bonus case every evening from 8 PM.

              Case of the Week

              Each Daily from Monday to Saturday gives you one clue to a bigger case. On Sunday, use all 6 clues to name the mastermind. You get one try.

              Streaks, shields and keys

              Play the Daily every day to grow your streak. A Streak Shield saves it if you miss a day. A Case Key opens a Daily you missed, with full rewards and its weekly clue. You earn one of each every 7 days in a row.

              Keyboard: keys 1-5 pick, Enter confirms, Esc cancels.
