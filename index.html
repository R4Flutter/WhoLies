        How to play

        The police checked some facts. They're true. Then three suspects tell you their story.

        One story can't be true. Compare each one with the facts and tap the liar.

        Twist cases

        Every 5th case changes the rules. Sometimes two people are lying and you must find the honest one. Sometimes everyone is honest and one fact is fake.
